# Krost brand pack: logos and identity guide

The official Krost logo pack. Marketing owns it. Use only these files, never logos copied from old decks, signatures or website caches.

- `01 Logos/02 Web Email RGB/`: PNG, JPG and GIF for screens, email, decks and documents (Krost, Krost Bus Furn vertical, K; black and white).
- `01 Logos/01 Print CMYK/`: AI, EPS, TIF and JPG for printers and signage (Krost, Krost Bus Furn horizontal and vertical, K; black and white).
- `02 InDesign files/`: business card, envelope and letterhead (INDD plus PDFs).
- `Krost Identity Guide.pdf`: the full identity rules (October 2011).

Key rules: the logo is black or white only, on white, black or Krost lime (CMYK 12/0/100/9, RGB 212/211/19). Keep clear space equal to the height of the K. Never stretch, recolour, rotate or place it over images. The typeface is DIN. The tagline is "SOLUTIONS BEYOND SPACE AND TIME."
